About

Senior Health Economist and AI engineer with 10+ years bridging rigorous HEOR methodology, advanced analytics and software engineering. I have led HTA submissions to NICE, NCPE and SMC and built cost-effectiveness, budget impact, partitioned-survival, Markov and dynamic-transmission models across oncology, rare disease, cardiometabolic and renal conditions.

Since November 2025 I have delivered a portfolio of freelance HEOR models alongside production-grade generative-AI systems: retrieval-augmented evidence agents, multi-model LLM orchestration and automated evidence-to-deliverable pipelines. In 2026 I completed a Master's in Data Science & AI with Distinction.

My background is unusual for the field. I trained as an agricultural engineer, then as a health economist, then as a blockchain developer, and I have worked and studied in Spain, the UK, Switzerland, the Netherlands and Belgium.
